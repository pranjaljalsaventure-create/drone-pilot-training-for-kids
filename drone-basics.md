# Drone Basics

Welcome to the **Drone Basics** repository! This guide is designed for beginners who want to understand the fundamentals of drone technology. Whether you're a student, hobbyist, or aspiring drone pilot, this repository provides a simple introduction to drones, their components, applications, safety guidelines, and future opportunities.

---

##What is a Drone?

A drone, also known as an **Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV)**, is an aircraft that operates without a pilot on board. Drones are controlled remotely or can fly autonomously using GPS, sensors, and onboard software.

Today, drones are widely used across industries because they can capture aerial images, collect data, inspect infrastructure, and perform tasks that may be difficult or dangerous for humans.

---

## Main Components of a Drone

A typical drone consists of the following parts:

- **Frame** – The main body that holds all components.
- **Motors** – Generate the power needed for flight.
- **Propellers** – Lift and move the drone.
- **Flight Controller** – Acts as the drone's brain.
- **Battery** – Supplies power to the drone.
- **GPS Module** – Enables navigation and positioning.
- **Camera** – Captures photos and videos.
- **Remote Controller** – Used to operate the drone.

## Basic Drone Safety Tips

Always follow these safety practices before flying:

- Fly in open and safe locations.
- Check weather conditions before takeoff.
- Keep the drone within your visual line of sight.
- Avoid airports and restricted areas.
- Ensure the battery is fully charged.
- Perform a pre-flight equipment check.
- Follow local drone regulations.
